Common questions

How it works

Who are the student advisors?

Current university students or recent graduates who went through the same application process and decisions you're facing. They share real experience about what it's actually like.

Who are the career experts?

Professionals with 10+ years of experience hiring, managing teams, or building careers in their field. They provide market perspective and strategic guidance.

How do I book a session?

Select a plan, complete payment, and you'll be matched with an advisor based on your profile and goals. You'll receive their availability within 24 hours.

What if I need to reschedule?

You can reschedule up to 48 hours before your session at no charge. Sessions cancelled with less notice forfeit 50% of the fee.

Are sessions online or in person?

All sessions are conducted online via video call, making it easy to connect with advisors anywhere in the world.

Can I get a refund?

If you're not satisfied with your first session, contact us within 48 hours for a full refund. Subsequent sessions are non-refundable but can be rescheduled.
